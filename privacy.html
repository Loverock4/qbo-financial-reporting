Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the private, internal QuickBooks Online integration ("the Application") operated by 2568551 Ontario Inc. ("we", "us").

Who uses the Application

The Application is for internal company use only. It is operated by the company and its authorized personnel and is not made available to the public or to any external users.

What data we access

The Application connects to our own QuickBooks Online company account via Intuit's official API to read accounting information — such as financial reports, accounts, transactions, and company details. Access is read-only; the Application does not write, alter, or delete any data in QuickBooks.

How we store and protect data

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely and privately on the operator's own system with restricted file permissions. Retrieved accounting data is used only for the company's internal purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share any Intuit data with third parties.

Data sharing

No Intuit data accessed by the Application is shared beyond the company that owns the QuickBooks account. There are no third-party recipients.

Data retention and removal

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Application from within QuickBooks Online or by deleting the stored credentials on the operator's system.
