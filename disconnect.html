Disconnect & Reconnect

This private, internal QuickBooks Online integration is operated by 2568551 Ontario Inc. for its own company account.

How to disconnect

To revoke the Application's access, sign in to QuickBooks Online, go to Settings → Apps (or the Intuit account's connected-apps page), locate this application, and choose Disconnect. Access is revoked immediately and stored credentials on the operator's system can then be deleted.

How to reconnect

To restore access after disconnecting or after credentials expire, the operator re-runs the Application's authorization flow and grants access again through Intuit's standard OAuth consent screen.
