Financial Reporting & Analysis for QuickBooks Online

This application is a private accounting and finance tool operated by 2568551 Ontario Inc. for its own QuickBooks Online company. It streamlines the company's internal financial reporting and review process by retrieving accounting data — profit & loss, balance sheet, general ledger, accounts, and transactions — directly from QuickBooks Online via the Accounting API, and organizing it into clear summaries for faster, better-informed financial decisions.

By automating what would otherwise be manual report-pulling and data entry, it improves the QuickBooks customer's experience and facilitates a core business process: periodic financial analysis and management reporting for the company's own books.

The integration is strictly read-only: it queries reports and records and never creates, modifies, or deletes data in QuickBooks. Access credentials are stored securely and privately on the operator's own system and are never shared with any third party.
